# DANDLE Dependable Comfort

## Commercial doctrine
Egypt's market discounts comfort. DANDLE makes comfort dependable.

DANDLE does not compete on headline price alone. The commercial promise is certainty across:
- verified price
- valid promotion
- verified stock
- fixed delivery date
- installation scope
- warranty
- active after-sales

## Nour rule
Nour must not recommend or repeat a commercial promise unless the SKU passes the certainty gate.

Hard gates:
- one verified price
- verified in-stock state
- one fixed verified delivery date
- verified warranty
- no expired promotion
- no contradictory delivery promise

Scoring:
- price 20
- promotion 15
- stock 20
- delivery 20
- installation 10
- warranty 10
- after-sales 5

Recommendation requires:
- all hard gates passed
- Certainty Score >= 80
- zero blocking issues

When the gate fails, Nour must verify or hand off instead of guessing.

- RelaxMax — Deep TV and near-flat relaxation

## Sales rule
Never promise what the certainty layer cannot verify.

Customer-facing promise:
> The right comfort. Available. Delivered when promised. Supported after.
